This is an exercise in using an AI workflow to engage in statistical analysis of a pre-generated experiment and dataset. Your role will be to review the experimental design description and dataset, decide on the appropriate statistical tests to run, analyze the data using your statistical software of choice, and then write up a brief report presenting your statistical analysis. The goal is to give you a beginning-to-end experience of doing a statistical analysis. This exercise works in two stages, with two sets of folders marked stage 1 and stage 2. In stage 1, you will upload the content of the stage 1 folder (ai-orientation-1.md, data.csv, experimental-description.md) to your AI of choice. The AI's role is to provide with assistance running the tests and writing the report. The AI should help with using the statistical software, enabling you to run your chosen tests, interpreting errors and outputs. The AI should not help you with making decisions about which methods are appropriate, analyzing the dataset, or hinting towards the correct answer. 



For stage 1, you will upload the appropriate documents and then review experimental-description.md and data.csv. From the design description, you will choose the appropriate method to analyze the data. You will then decide on which variables, if any, should be excluded from the analysis for design reasons. You will then check that the relevant statistical assumptions are met for your chosen method, note any existing complications in the data, fit the model using your statistical software of choice, and then write the report. The report should include



1. the design,
2. your chosen method of analysis with justification,
3. a decision to include or exclude for each variable with justification,
4. checks of the relevant assumptions for your chosen method,
5. results with effect sizes and confidence intervals, and
6. interpretation of those results with respect to the initial experimental design.



Once the report is written, you should upload the report and the contents of the stage 2 folder (solution-key.md, experimental-description.md, data.csv, ai-orientation-2.md) to a new AI instance. This is to avoid prior discussion with the first AI instance from influencing the analysis of your report. Once the report is uploaded, the new AI instance will evaluate your report against the solution key. The AI will provide some hints as to errors in the analysis, if any exist, and then give you one opportunity to find and revise the errors. If revision is necessary, make the revisions and reupload the revised report. The AI will then reveal the contents of the solution key, included which variables should have been excluded, what complications existed in the data (if any), the results of the statistical tests and model fitting, and the intended interpretation of those results. 



This project is expected to be completed in 20-60 minutes depending on your fluency with statistical analysis and your chosen statistical software. While you have access to the solution key in order to upload it to the AI in stage 2, you are strongly encouraged not to look at it before it is revealed at the end of the exercise.
